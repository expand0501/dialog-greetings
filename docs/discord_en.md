## Description
Stalkers greet you when you **start talking** to them, not every time you walk past.

## Features
- Open a dialog with a stalker and he says one of his own greeting lines, in his voice, with subtitles.
- No more "hello" every time you pass within 3 m of a stalker. MCM configurable (on by default).
- No more "put your weapon away" / "stop" lines when you walk up with a weapon drawn. MCM configurable (on by default).
- Hostile and wounded stalkers stay silent. So do traders and anyone without greeting voice lines.
- Companions stay silent unless you enable "Companions greet too" in MCM.
- Greeting chance (0-100%) and the delay before the same stalker greets you again (default 60 s, so reopening a dialog after trading doesn't repeat it) are MCM configurable. All settings apply instantly.
- No replaced game scripts, no per-frame work.

## Install / remove
- Standalone: no other mods required, MCM is optional (defaults are used without it).
- **Safe to install and remove in the middle of a playthrough**: the mod saves nothing and adds no items or NPCs. No new game needed.
- No known conflicts.
